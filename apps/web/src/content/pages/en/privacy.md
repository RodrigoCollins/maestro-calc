# Privacy

This page describes what Maestro Calc does with information today. It covers only what the site does now.

## Hosting

Maestro Calc is hosted on Cloudflare. When you load a page, Cloudflare processes standard request logs, which can include your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser type.

## Analytics and cookies

Maestro Calc uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see which pages people use. It starts after the page has loaded. The site has no advertising and no accounts.

Google Analytics receives the page you visit without the values you typed, your browser and device type, your screen size and an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, it can set first-party cookies, such as `_ga`, to tell one visit from another.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Analytics sets no cookie: it only receives a signal that a page was viewed. The same applies anywhere when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. Advertising storage and ad personalization are off everywhere.

You can also block cookies in your browser or install Google's opt-out add-on for Google Analytics.

## Calculators

Calculators run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or to Google Analytics. The values you enter sit in the page address, so you can copy the link and share it. Opening or sharing that address sends it like any page request, so it can appear in Cloudflare's request logs. The address with your values also stays in your browser history, like any page you visit.

## Email

If you write to contacto@maestrocalc.com, we use your message and your address only to reply to you.
