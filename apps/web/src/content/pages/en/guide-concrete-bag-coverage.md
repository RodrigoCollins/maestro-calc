# How much does a bag of concrete cover?

At 4 in thick, one bag of concrete mix covers about 1.80 square feet (80 lb bag), 1.35 square feet (60 lb) or 0.90 square feet (40 lb). A thicker slab spreads the same bag over less ground, so start with the thickness, then count the bags.

## What one bag makes

A bag of concrete mix makes approximately 0.60 cubic feet of concrete (80 lb bag), 0.45 cubic feet (60 lb) or 0.30 cubic feet (40 lb), according to the manufacturer's data sheet. The yield is in proportion to the weight: two 40 lb bags make what one 80 lb bag makes.

## Square feet per bag

Divide the yield by the thickness in feet. 4 in is 1/3 ft, so a bag covers three times its yield in square feet; 6 in is 1/2 ft, so it covers twice its yield:

| Bag   | At 4 in thick | At 6 in thick |
| ----- | ------------- | ------------- |
| 80 lb | 1.80 sq ft    | 1.20 sq ft    |
| 60 lb | 1.35 sq ft    | 0.90 sq ft    |
| 40 lb | 0.90 sq ft    | 0.60 sq ft    |

These are full bags with nothing lost. Some mix always stays in the wheelbarrow, and the ground is never perfectly flat, so plan on some extra.

## Bags for a slab

A slab 10 ft by 10 ft, 4 in thick, is 10 × 10 × 1/3 = 33.33 cubic feet. At 0.60 cubic feet a bag, that is 55.6 bags of 80 lb: 56 bags, with no extra.

With the 10 percent extra the [concrete calculator](/concrete-calculator) adds by default, the slab takes 36.67 cubic feet, or 1.36 cubic yards:

- 62 bags of 80 lb,
- 82 bags of 60 lb, or
- 123 bags of 40 lb.

At 6 in thick, the same slab with the extra takes 55.00 cubic feet: 92 bags of 80 lb. The calculator rounds the bags up only once, at the end, so the count never comes up short.

## Which bag size to buy

Each size makes the same concrete per pound, so the bag count changes and the concrete does not. An 80 lb bag means the fewest bags to open and mix; a 40 lb bag is easier to carry and to load. Enter your store's price per bag in the calculator to compare the totals of each size.

## When bags stop making sense

One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet: about 45 bags of 80 lb. Above a cubic yard or two, a ready-mix truck is often simpler; the guide [Concrete: bags or a truck?](/guides/concrete-bags-or-truck) compares the two. The guide [How to calculate cubic yards](/guides/how-to-calculate-cubic-yards) explains the volume math, inches to feet and circles included.
