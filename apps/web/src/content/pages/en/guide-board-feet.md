# How to calculate board feet

To calculate board feet, multiply the thickness in inches by the width in inches and by the length in feet, then divide by 12. A 2x4 that is 8 ft long is 2 × 4 × 8 ÷ 12 = 5.33 board feet. That is the board measure of the American Softwood Lumber Standard, DOC PS 20-20, section 2.2, the rule lumberyards tally softwood by.

## What a board foot is

A board foot is a piece of wood 1 inch thick, 12 inches wide and 1 foot long: 144 cubic inches, or one twelfth of a cubic foot. It measures volume, not area, so a 2 in plank covers half the floor of a 1 in board with the same board feet. Twelve board feet stack into one cubic foot.

## The formula, step by step

1. Write down the thickness and the width in inches. For framing lumber, use the size in its name: a 2x6 is 2 in by 6 in.
2. Write down the length in feet. Lumber is sold in even lengths, 8, 10, 12, 14 and 16 ft.
3. Multiply the three numbers and divide by 12. The ÷ 12 turns the width in inches into feet, as the standard's rule takes it.
4. For several pieces of the same size, multiply by the number of pieces. For a mixed order, work out each size and add them.

Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12.

## Worked examples

- **A 2x6, 10 ft long:** 2 × 6 × 10 ÷ 12 = 120 ÷ 12 = 10.00 board feet.
- **A 4x4 post, 8 ft long:** 4 × 4 × 8 ÷ 12 = 128 ÷ 12 = 10.67 board feet.
- **Ten 2x4x8 studs:** 10 × 64 ÷ 12 = 53.33 board feet.
- **A 1x6 board, 8 ft long:** 1 × 6 × 8 ÷ 12 = 4.00 board feet.
- **A hardwood board 8/4 thick, 6 in wide and 10 ft long:** 8/4 is 2 in, so 2 × 6 × 10 ÷ 12 = 10.00 board feet.

## Nominal or actual size?

A 2x4 measures about 1 1/2 by 3 1/2 in once it is dried and planed: PS 20-20, Table 3, sets that as its minimum dry dressed size. The board measure rule takes the nominal size, so a 2x4x8 is tallied at 5.33 board feet although the wood in it, at its dressed size, is 3.50 board feet. Use the nominal size to check a tally or a price; use the measured size when you need the actual volume of wood, such as for its weight.

| Nominal size | Dry dressed size  | Board feet per linear foot (nominal) |
| ------------ | ----------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1x6          | 3/4 × 5 1/2 in    | 0.50                                 |
| 2x4          | 1 1/2 × 3 1/2 in  | 0.67                                 |
| 2x6          | 1 1/2 × 5 1/2 in  | 1.00                                 |
| 2x8          | 1 1/2 × 7 1/4 in  | 1.33                                 |
| 2x10         | 1 1/2 × 9 1/4 in  | 1.67                                 |
| 2x12         | 1 1/2 × 11 1/4 in | 2.00                                 |
| 4x4          | 3 1/2 × 3 1/2 in  | 1.33                                 |

Multiply the last column by the length in feet to get the board feet of a piece: a 2x8 16 ft long is 1.33 × 16, which is 21.33 board feet worked exactly (2 × 8 × 16 ÷ 12).

## From a price per piece to a price per board foot

To compare a yard that prices by the piece with one that prices by the board foot, divide the price of the piece by its board feet. A 2x6x10 is 10 board feet, so its price per piece is ten times its price per board foot. Compare prices only for the same species and grade, and ask whether a hardwood tally takes the rough or the surfaced thickness.

These are estimates. Confirm the tally with your supplier before you order.
