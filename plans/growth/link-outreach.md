# Link outreach research: Maestro Calc, Cuenta Horas, Pool Figures

Research date: 2026-11-03. Research only: nothing was submitted, posted or emailed.

Site codes: **MC** = maestrocalc.com, **CH** = cuentahoras.com, **PF** = poolfigures.com.

Status key:

- **verified (fetched)**: the page was fetched on 2026-11-03 and the notes come from it.
- **verified (search)**: the page showed up in a search result on 2026-11-03, but fetching it failed or was not tried. The notes come from the result snippet.
- **unverified**: the reason is given.

This repository is public, so personal names and personal email addresses are left out: use the
contact the page itself names. Generic organization addresses are kept.

No traffic, domain-authority or audience numbers are included. Where a page quotes its own numbers, they are left out on purpose.

Total: **38 targets** (30 verified, 8 unverified). Per site, counting the 7 general directories that fit all three: MC 18, CH 19, PF 15.

## Excluded and why

- **AlternativeTo**: its FAQ (fetched) declines "simple online tools — PDF converters, calculators, URL shorteners" and apps limited to one country or to some US states. Listing is free, with an optional paid priority review that "does not buy approval".
- **BetaList**: its criteria (fetched) require "a technology startup" that is new to their audience, with a sign-up or access path. Free single-purpose calculators don't fit. Reports on pricing conflict, and some say every submission is paid.
- **Fazier**: third-party sources disagree on whether it is still free. Not verified, so left out.
- **Bulk "submit to N directories" services** (for example the "30 named directories, 24-hour turnaround" listing services that showed up in search): paid bulk submission, link-farm risk.
- **AI-tool directories** (SubmitAITools, NewTool.site and similar): off-topic audience, and many push paid tiers.
- **Trouble Free Pool / Pool Math, Swim University, Orenda (Rule Your Pool), PoolDial, Hayward, Pentair**: each runs its own competing pool calculators or app, so a link is unlikely. Hayward and Pentair are manufacturers.
- **Texas DSHS, Minnesota Dept. of Health and City of Minneapolis pool pages**: they cover public pools and link only government bodies and standards. Texas has an endorsement disclaimer.
- **Poolweb Help Desk blog** (poolwebhelp.blogspot.com): no posts since March 2016, and its calculator list links only Pentair tools.
- **Above Ground Pool Central (carrd page)**: no outside links and no contact.
- **Nebraska Extension Lancaster "Garden Math" and UW-Madison "Do the Math in Yards & Gardens"**: fetched, and neither links any outside calculator.
- **Mohawk College trades math guide** (404) and **Buildipedia "Helpful Online Tools"** (domain no longer resolves): dead.
- **One Fair Wage**: no resources or links page with tools was found. It is an advocacy organization.
- **Reddit**: out of scope by instruction.

## Suggested order (highest odds first)

1. Iowa State Yard and Garden (#8): it already links outside mulch calculators.
2. Community Tax Aid (#21) and UNG (#27): they already link outside calculators.
3. Voz (#19), NJ State Library (#22), Working for Dignity (#20): Spanish-language worker or community resource pages with named contacts.
4. Camosun (#9) and Olympic College (#10): library guides that list calculators.
5. Launch platforms (#1-#5), each site on a separate day or week.
