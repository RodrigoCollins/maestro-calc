# Proposal: guide-fence-post-concrete

## Intent

The concrete hub's third guide, ending in the concrete calculator: "how much concrete for a fence post", a frequent search the calculator answers only in part (its column shape gives the hole, not the post or the soil on top). A mulch guide and a flooring-waste guide were weighed first and dropped: the mulch page already covers depth, trunks and coverage, and the flooring waste percentages found online trace to calculator sites, not to a primary source.

## Decisions

1. **One primary source**: the Quikrete Fast-Setting Concrete Mix data sheet (revised November 2025): hole about 3 times the post's width and 1/3 of its length deep, dry mix to 3 to 4 in from the top, about 1 gal of water per 50 lb bag, fill in two lifts past 30 in, sets in 20 to 40 minutes, 4 hours before any strain, yields of 0.375 ft³ (50 lb) and 0.45 ft³ (60 lb), and the two-bag case (3 in square post, 9 in hole, 30 in deep).
2. **Worked example** from that case: hole 1.10 ft³, post 0.16 ft³, concrete to 4 in below grade 0.82 ft³, 2.2 bags of 50 lb or 1.8 of 60 lb; the sheet's "two bags" is stated as its rounding, with a spare bag advised; 10 posts 8.2 ft³, 22 bags of 50 lb or 19 of 60 lb.
3. **The calculator**: the column shape with pieces = posts gives the hole volume with the extra, an upper bound; its bag counts are regular concrete mix, so the guide says to divide by the yield on a fast-setting bag.
4. Same frame as the other guides; counts 36 ids, 72 URLs, 74 pages.
