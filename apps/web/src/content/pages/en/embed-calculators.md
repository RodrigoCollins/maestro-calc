# Embed our calculators

You can put any Maestro Calc calculator on your own website for free. It works the same as on our site: the numbers, the drawing, the metric units and the link your visitors can share.

## How to embed a calculator

1. Find the calculator below and select its whole code.
2. Paste the code into your page where you want the calculator to show. Most site builders call this an HTML or embed block.
3. Keep the link under the calculator. It tells your visitors where the numbers come from, and it is the only thing we ask in return.

The calculator opens at the size of your page. If it looks too short or too tall, change the number after `height` in the code.

The embedded calculator does not track your visitors and loads no ads. Its numbers come from the same formulas and sources as our own pages, described in our [methodology](/methodology).

Questions or a calculator you need? Write to us at [contacto@maestrocalc.com](mailto:contacto@maestrocalc.com).
