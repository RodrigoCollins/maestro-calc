<h2 id="how-to-use">How to use the feet and inches calculator</h2>

The calculator does tape-measure math: it adds or subtracts two lengths in feet, inches and fractions, multiplies or divides one length by a whole number, or tells you how many pieces of one length fit in another. To turn a decimal into feet and inches, type it and multiply it by 1: 11.65 ft is 11 ft 7 13/16 in. The result updates as you type, so there is nothing to submit.

1. Type the first length the way you read it on the tape: 12 ft 6 in, 12' 6", 6 1/2 in or 75.5 in. A plain number is read in feet.
2. Choose the operation: Add, Subtract, Multiply, Divide or How many fit.
3. To add, subtract or see how many fit, type the second length. To multiply or divide, type the number: 3 to triple a length, 4 to cut a board into four equal pieces.

The result leads with feet, inches and the fraction to the nearest 1/16 of an inch, the mark you find on a tape. It also shows the result in decimal inches and in meters. When you subtract a longer length from a shorter one, the calculator gives the difference and says which length is longer. How many fit answers with the whole pieces and the quotient, and the figures show the length left over.

To work in metric, press Metric (m, cm). Lengths are then read in meters, and the result leads with meters. Switching units never changes your numbers, only how they are written. <h2 id="formulas">Formulas and worked example</h2>

The calculator turns every length into inches, does the operation exactly, and writes the result back in feet and inches:

- Add: first + second.
- Subtract: first − second; when the second length is longer, second − first.
- Multiply: first × number.
- Divide: first ÷ number.
- How many fit: first ÷ second; the whole pieces are its whole part, and the length left over is first − pieces × second.
- Feet and inches: whole feet = inches ÷ 12, rounded down; the rest stays in inches, rounded to the nearest 1/16 of an inch.

Worked example: add 3 ft 8 1/4 in to 12 ft 6 in.

- 12 ft 6 in is 12 × 12 + 6 = 150 inches, and 3 ft 8 1/4 in is 36 + 8.25 = 44.25 inches.
- 150 + 44.25 = 194.25 inches.
- 194.25 ÷ 12 = 16 whole feet, and 194.25 − 192 = 2.25 inches, so the result is 16 ft 2 1/4 in.
- In meters, 194.25 × 0.0254 = 4.934 meters.

The calculator keeps exact fractions from the first input to the last step. Only the feet-and-inches figure is rounded, to the nearest 1/16 of an inch, and the other figures are rounded when they are shown. <h2 id="tips">Tips and common mistakes</h2>

Read the tape to the smallest mark you trust. Most tapes mark 1/16 of an inch; a cut is rarely better than that, so the calculator rounds there.

Account for the saw blade when you divide a board. Dividing 12 ft 6 in by 7 gives seven pieces of 1 ft 9 7/16 in, but each cut removes about 1/8 of an inch of wood. Measure and cut one piece at a time, or subtract the cuts first.

Type feet and inches together. 5 ft 3 1/2 in is one length; 5 3 1/2 is not. When in doubt, write the units: ft, in, ' or ".

Check the operation before you cut. The answer repeats both lengths in words, so read it once: it is the quickest way to catch a slip.

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>

### How do I add feet and inches?

Add the inches, carry every 12 inches to the feet, then add the feet. The calculator does it exactly: 12 ft 6 in plus 3 ft 8 1/4 in is 16 ft 2 1/4 in.

### How do I subtract feet and inches?

Turn both lengths into inches, subtract, and write the result back in feet and inches. 12 ft 6 in minus 3 ft 8 1/4 in is 8 ft 9 3/4 in.

### How do I divide a length into equal parts?

Choose Divide and type the number of parts. 12 ft 6 in divided into 7 equal parts is 1 ft 9 7/16 in each, to the nearest 1/16 of an inch.

### Why is the result rounded to 1/16 of an inch?

It is the smallest mark on most tapes. The decimal inches and the meters show the exact value to two and three places.

### How do I convert decimal feet to feet and inches?

Keep the whole feet and multiply the decimal part by 12 to get the inches. 5.75 ft is 5 ft plus 0.75 × 12 = 9 in, so 5 ft 9 in. When the inches are not whole, round them to the nearest 1/16 of an inch: 5.3 ft is 5 ft 3.6 in, which is 5 ft 3 5/8 in on the tape.

### How do I convert inches to decimal feet?

Divide the inches by 12. 7 in is 7 ÷ 12 = 0.583 ft, and 5 ft 7 in is 5.583 ft. The table in the tips above lists 1 to 11 inches as decimal feet.

### How many inches are in a foot?

Twelve. A foot is exactly 12 inches, and an inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters.

### How many 16-inch pieces fit in 20 feet?

Fifteen. Choose How many fit, type 20 ft and 16 in: 20 ft is 240 inches, and 240 ÷ 16 = 15, so 15 whole pieces with nothing left over. With 7-inch pieces, 240 ÷ 7 = 34.28: 34 whole pieces and 2 inches left over. Leave room for the saw blade: each cut takes about 1/8 of an inch.
