# How to calculate square footage

Square footage is the area of a flat surface measured in square feet: how many 1 ft by 1 ft squares fit on it. Flooring, tile, sod and paint coverage are sold or rated by it, so it is often the first number of a job.

## A rectangle: length times width

Measure the two sides in feet and multiply them. A room 12 ft by 14 ft 6 in is 12 × 14.5 = 174 square feet.

Turn inches into feet before you multiply: 6 inches is 6 ÷ 12 = 0.5 ft. If you measured both sides in inches, multiply them and divide by 144, the square inches in a square foot: 150 in × 96 in = 14,400 square inches, or 100 square feet.

## Shapes made of rectangles

Most rooms that are not rectangles are a few rectangles put together. Split the shape along a wall line, work out each part and add them. An L-shaped area made of a 20 ft by 12 ft part and an 8 ft by 10 ft part is 240 + 80 = 320 square feet.

Measure each part once, and do not count the corner where two parts meet twice. For an area cut out of a room, such as a fixed counter, work it out the same way and subtract it.

A triangle is half a rectangle: half its base times its height. A triangle 10 ft along the base and 6 ft high is 10 × 6 ÷ 2 = 30 square feet.

## Square yards and square meters

- A square yard is 3 ft by 3 ft, which is 9 square feet. Carpet is often sold by it: 174 square feet is 174 ÷ 9, approximately 19.33 square yards.
- A foot is exactly 0.3048 meters, so a square foot is exactly 0.3048 × 0.3048 = 0.09290304 square meters: 174 square feet is approximately 16.17 square meters.
- To go back, divide by the same number. A square meter is approximately 10.764 square feet, so 20 square meters is approximately 215.28 square feet.

## Before you buy

The area is what the surface measures, not what you have to buy. Materials are cut to fit and sold in whole boxes, rolls or bags, so check the extra the product's maker recommends for cuts and waste, and round up to whole units.
