# Roofing tools

Start with the pitch: the inches a roof rises for every 12 inches it runs. The roof pitch calculator turns a rise and a run into the angle and the rafter length, and checks the pitch against the minimum slope the 2021 International Residential Code sets for your covering.

The roofing calculator then turns the footprint and the pitch into the roof area in squares, the bundles of shingles and the rolls of underlayment. A square is 100 square feet.

The second guide prices the job: the bundles and the underlayment, everything around the shingles, the work, and what goes in writing.
