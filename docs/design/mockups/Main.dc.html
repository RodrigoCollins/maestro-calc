How to use it

  1. Measure length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Enter the slab thickness in inches. Driveways usually run 4″ to 6″.
  3. Keep 10% extra unless the base is perfectly graded.
  4. Pick the bag size you buy, or compare with a truck order.

The formula

Cubic yards = length × width × thickness ÷ 27

Thickness goes in feet: 4″ is 4 ÷ 12 = 0.333 ft. A 12 × 10 ft slab at 4″ is 12 × 10 × 0.333 = 40 ft³, and 40 ÷ 27 = 1.48 yd³ before extra.
