# Proposal: guide-gravel-driveway

## Intent

The landscaping hub had three calculators and no guide, the thinnest of the hubs. A driveway guide ending in the gravel calculator (49.5K searches a month in English, PLAN §4.1) answers "how much gravel do I need for a driveway" without adding a calculator, so it stays inside the PLAN §11.3 gate.

## Decisions

1. **A plan, not the calculator again.** The calculator page already works one 20 × 10 ft layer. The guide covers what it does not: building in layers (each at least twice the top-size stone, FHWA), one layer per run of the calculator, ordering loose cubic yards rather than the volume in place, surface gravel with fines that binds (FHWA), a 4 percent crown of about 1/2 in per foot (FHWA) and fabric over wet, soft soil (FHWA). The worked example is a 60 × 10 ft top layer 4 in deep, compacted, 5 percent extra: 14.23 tons, 11.03 loose cubic yards, with the calculator's WisDOT densities. No total depth and no price is given.
2. **Same frame as the other guides**: `ROUTES`, `TRUST_IDS`, `GUIDES` tied to `gravel`, the landscaping hub's first guide, site catalog keys, end-state counts (33 ids, 66 URLs, 68 pages). The glossary gains "crown" → "bombeo".
