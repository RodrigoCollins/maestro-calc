# Editorial policy

This page explains who makes Maestro Calc, where its numbers come from, how we check them and how to tell us when something is wrong.

<!-- Raw h2 elements keep each section anchor stable if a heading is reworded. -->

<h2 id="who-we-are">Who we are</h2>

Maestro Calc is made by a small independent team that builds free jobsite calculators in English and Spanish. Every page is signed by the Maestro Calc editorial team, not by a person. We are not contractors, engineers or building inspectors, and we do not claim credentials we do not hold. We build the calculators, test them and keep their sources up to date. More about us and about how we use AI is on the [about page](/about).

<h2 id="sources">Where the numbers come from</h2>

Every constant a calculator uses comes from a published source:

- Data sheets and product listings from the manufacturer: bag yields, coverage per gallon, bundles per square, roll sizes.
- The International Residential Code, 2021 edition, for roof slopes and underlayment.
- NIST SP 811 for every conversion between units.
- Published state and federal tables for the weight of gravel, sand and soil.

Each calculator page links its own sources in its last section, with the day we read them. The full list, with links, is at the end of this page.

<h2 id="checks">How we check the figures</h2>

Calculations use exact numbers from the first input to the last step, and a result is rounded once, when it is shown. Every formula has automated tests built on cases worked out by hand, and the [methodology page](/methodology) publishes those cases with the formulas, so you can check them yourself. A change that breaks one of those cases is not published.

<h2 id="reviews">When we review pages</h2>

Every calculator page, guide and chart shows the day it was last reviewed. We review a calculator again when one of its sources changes, such as a new data sheet or a new code edition, and we recheck every source at least once a year.

<h2 id="corrections">How to report an error</h2>

Found a wrong number or an outdated source? Write to contacto@maestrocalc.com with the page and what you found. We review every report, fix confirmed errors and log each correction with its date on the [about page](/about#corrections).

<h2 id="no-advice">Not professional advice</h2>

Our figures are estimates to help you plan and buy. They are not professional advice, and they do not replace your local building code, the instructions on the product you buy or a licensed professional who sees the job.

<h2 id="affiliate-links">Affiliate disclosure</h2>

Some links on Maestro Calc may be paid (affiliate) links. When you sign up or buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Each paid link is labelled "Paid link" right next to it.

We recommend a tool only when it is one we would recommend for the task on that page, whether or not it pays. Payment never changes a calculator result, a figure or a ranking, and we do not show prices that can go stale.
