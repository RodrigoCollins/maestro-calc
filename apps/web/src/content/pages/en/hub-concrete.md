# Concrete tools

Work out a pour before you buy: how many cubic yards, how many bags, and what it costs. The concrete calculator covers slabs, footings, columns and steps, adds the extra you choose for waste, and gives the result in cubic yards, cubic feet, cubic meters and bags of 40, 60 or 80 lb.

One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet: about 45 bags of 80 lb. The guides below help you decide between bags and a ready-mix truck, and turn the numbers into a quote your client can check.
