# Proposal: guide-raised-bed-soil

## Intent

The landscaping hub's second guide, ending in the topsoil calculator: "how much soil for a raised bed", a common search the calculator page mentions but does not answer (how deep, with what, how much of each). A mulch guide was weighed first and dropped: the mulch page already answers depth, bags or bulk and extra, so a guide would repeat it.

## Decisions

1. **Sourced to two university extensions.** Depth by crop on pavement or compacted soil (at least 8 in for leafy greens, beans and cucumbers; 12 to 24 in for peppers, tomatoes and squash) and the mixes for a bed on a lawn (compost to topsoil 1:2 or 1:1) and on pavement (compost and soilless mix 1:1, topsoil up to a fifth only from 16 in deep): University of Maryland Extension. Equal thirds or 70/30 and filling in worked-in layers: Iowa State University Extension. No settling figure (neither source gives one) beyond the calculator's own 10 percent extra.
2. **Worked example**: 8 × 4 ft, 12 in deep: 32 ft³, 35.20 ft³ with 10 percent (1.30 yd³); at 1:2, 23.47 ft³ of topsoil (16 bags of 1.5 ft³) and 11.73 ft³ of compost (8 bags).
3. **Spanish terms**: "cama elevada" (as the topsoil page already says) and "composta" (the stores' word), both added to the glossary; crops in the Mexican usage (chiles, jitomates).
4. Same frame as the other guides; counts 34 ids, 68 URLs, 70 pages.
