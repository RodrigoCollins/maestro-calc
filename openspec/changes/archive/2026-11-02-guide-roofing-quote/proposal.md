# Proposal: guide-roofing-quote

## Intent

Roofing has the highest CPC of the PLAN §4.1 catalog ($11 to $13) and its hub had one guide. The roofing calculator and the pitch guide already answer "how many bundles" and "how to measure a roof"; what they leave out, and the calculator says it does not count, is the rest of a roofing price. A quote guide, the fourth after concrete, drywall and paint, fills that gap.

## Decisions

1. **Only figures the site already sources.** Bundle coverage (GAF, 33.33 sq ft), the underlayment roll (GAF FeltBuster, about 957 sq ft in one layer with the code laps), one layer from 4 in 12, two from 2 to 4 in 12, no asphalt shingles below 2 in 12, and the ice barrier to 24 in inside the wall line (IRC 2021 R905), all as the roofing calculator cites them. The worked example is the calculator's (40 × 30 ft at 6 in 12: 13.42 squares, 45 bundles, 2 rolls) plus the 15 percent hip-roof case (47 bundles). The IRC site refuses automated reads, so no new code figure is added: drip edge, starter, caps, flashing, nails and permits are lines of the quote with no figure, sent to the shingle maker's instructions and the local code.
2. **The glossary.** "roofing square" was "square" in the glossary while the whole Spanish site says "cuadrado"; the glossary now says "cuadrado", and gains hip (limatesa), valley (limahoya), drip edge (gotero), starter strip (tira de arranque), flashing (tapajuntas) and ice barrier (barrera contra hielo), the terms the Spanish roofing page already uses.
3. Same frame as the other guides; the roofing hub's second guide; counts 35 ids, 70 URLs, 72 pages.
