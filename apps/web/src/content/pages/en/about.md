# About Maestro Calc

Maestro Calc builds jobsite calculators that tell you exactly what to buy. We write in English and Spanish for builders and homeowners in the US.

Everything on this site comes from the Maestro Calc Editorial Team. We do not publish under personal names, and we do not claim credentials we do not hold.

<h2 id="editorial-policy">Editorial policy</h2>

Every page says what it covers and when it was last updated. We present estimates as estimates. The [methodology page](/methodology) explains how each number is worked out. The site has no advertising or sponsors today, so nothing here is shaped by one.

<h2 id="ai-use">How we use AI</h2>

Our content is AI-assisted and verified by people. We use AI tools to help write text and code. People review the text, every formula is tested against cases checked by hand, and sources are cited on each calculator page.

<h2 id="corrections">Corrections</h2>

Found an error? Write to contacto@maestrocalc.com. We review every report, fix confirmed errors and add each correction to a dated log on this page. No corrections have been logged yet.
