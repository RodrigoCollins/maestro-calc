# Affiliate programs: terms and rules

Every affiliate link on every site follows these rules. They come from the program terms the owner accepted. Breaking one can cost the account, the commissions already earned, or a legal claim (several programs make us indemnify them). When in doubt, do not place the link and ask the owner.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-15.

## Rules for every program

1. **Read the terms before the first link.** A program gets links only after its terms are summarized in the table below. If its terms are not in the table, the program has no links.
2. **Never accept terms without the owner's explicit OK.** Summarize the obligations (indemnity, liability cap, jurisdiction, restrictions) and wait for a clear yes.
3. **Never declare a false eligibility.** We are an individual resident in Argentina, not a US citizen, permanent resident or US entity. A program that requires any of those is skipped.
4. **Links only after approval**, and only the program's own tracking links, unmodified. No URL masking, no redirect that hides the destination, no link that looks like the brand's official site.
5. **Only on our own sites.** No affiliate links in social media, forums, newsgroups, comments, email or other people's sites unless the program's terms explicitly allow it.
6. **No paid search on brand terms.** We do not buy the brand, its products or misspellings as keywords, and we do not bid against the brand.
7. **No email campaigns** promoting a program without its written permission.
8. **FTC disclosure next to every affiliate link**, using the site-kit affiliate block (PR #396). No link without its disclosure.
9. **Truthful content only.** No claims the product does not support, no invented prices, discounts or coupons, no price changes or rebates of our own. Prices shown are dated and cited, or not shown.
10. **The brand's marks unaltered.** Use the program's approved logos and creatives as provided, or plain text names. Never imply the site is run, endorsed or sponsored by the brand ("affiliate" is the allowed wording).
11. **Respect the program's territory.** US-only programs get links only on pages for US readers.
12. **On termination**, remove the program's links and marks within the notice period (usually 7 days).

## Where links live

- Affiliate offers are declared per site in the `OFFERS` of the site-kit affiliate block (PR #396). An offer without approval stays with an empty URL; the page links to the brand's plain homepage meanwhile.
- Employer software (payroll, time tracking, scheduling) belongs on employer-facing Cuenta Horas pages, not on worker pages.
- Tax software belongs on tax pages for US readers, during the tax season.
