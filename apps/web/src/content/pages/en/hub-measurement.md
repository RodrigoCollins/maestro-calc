# Measurement tools

Most mistakes on a job start with a measurement. These calculators read lengths the way a tape measure does, in feet, inches and fractions: one adds, subtracts, multiplies or divides them, and the other turns a length and a width into square feet, square yards and square meters.

A foot is exactly 0.3048 meters, so a square foot is exactly 0.09290304 square meters. The guides show how to read every mark of a tape and how to find the area of a room that is not a rectangle.
