# Press sourcing: journalist request platforms for Maestro Calc, Cuenta Horas and Pool Figures

Research date: 2026-11-05. Research only: no account was created, nothing was submitted or sent.

Site codes: **MC** = maestrocalc.com (construction, EN and ES), **CH** = cuentahoras.com (hours and pay, EN and ES), **PF** = poolfigures.com (pool chemistry).

## Scope and relation to the outreach doc

`plans/growth/link-outreach.md` (2026-11-03) covers **proactive** outreach: 38 launch directories, library guides, extension pages, worker centers, trade associations and pool trade press, each pitched by us, with a suggested order. It does not cover journalist request platforms.

This doc covers the **reactive** channel: journalists post a query, we answer with a figure and a citable page. No target in the outreach doc is repeated here. Where an outlet appears in both (for example a trade magazine that also posts queries), the query answer follows this doc and the cold pitch follows the outreach doc.

## 2. Ranked recommendation

1. **Source of Sources** (free, three emails a day, strict on-topic rule that rewards our data-first answers). Start here.
2. **Qwoted Basic** (free, 2 pitches a month). Few pitches, but the reporters are the ones who write the personal finance and home stories CH and MC need. Spend the two pitches on the best CH and MC queries of the month.
3. Next, after four weeks of tracking: **HARO (relaunched)**, also free, as volume. Add it once the SOS routine takes under 15 minutes.
4. Later and only if the free tier proves out: **Featured** free tier for MC and CH Q&A prompts. Do not pay for any plan before the tracking table shows at least one published link from the free tiers.
5. Skip: MentionMatch (B2B only), Reportaro (Spain), Connectively (closed), Terkel (now Featured).

### Account steps Rodrigo does himself

Claude never creates accounts, signs up, verifies email or sends answers from an account.

1. Pick the sending address: a domain address (for example a press address on one of the three domains) reads better to journalists than a personal Gmail. Create it if it does not exist.
2. **Source of Sources**: go to sourceofsources.com, sign up as a source with that address, confirm the email, and add the sender to the inbox's safe list.
3. **Qwoted**: go to qwoted.com, sign up as an expert (Basic, free), fill the profile with the long bio below, add the three sites as companies or links, upload a headshot, choose topics (personal finance, labor and employment, home improvement, construction, pools), and request verification.
4. Set a mail filter that labels both senders `press-queries` so the daily routine reads one label.
5. Later (step 3 of the ranking): sign up at helpareporter.com with the same address.
6. Tell Claude which accounts exist so the routine and the tracking table can start.

## 6. Rules

1. **Real figure, real source.** Answer only when a live page holds the figure and cites its source. No opinions, no predictions, no figures we do not publish.
2. **Disclose.** Every answer says Rodrigo builds the site he links and names the credential he does not hold.
3. **No AI text sent unread.** Claude may draft; Rodrigo reads, edits and sends each answer himself from his own account.
4. **Maximum 3 responses a day** across all platforms (and Qwoted Basic's 2 a month). Quality over volume; SOS removes spammers with no appeal.
5. **One URL per answer**, the specific page. No attachments unless the query asks.
6. **Deadline first.** Skip a query whose deadline is under 2 hours away on Qwoted Basic (the 2-hour delay).
7. **Never pay for a placement**, and no paid plan before the table shows a published link from a free tier.
8. **Track every answer** in the table below the same day; check for publication weekly.
