# Proposal: guide-concrete-quote

## Intent

PLAN §5.4, guide 1: "How to quote a concrete job", EN/ES, ending in the concrete calculator. It is the sixth guide of seven and the second for concrete, so the concrete page now lists two guides ([GU8]).

## Decisions

1. **Same frame as the other guides.** `ROUTES` row, `TRUST_IDS` entry, `GUIDES` row tied to `concrete`, `page.<id>.title|description` in both site catalogs.
2. **No prices.** The quote is a structure: the concrete (the calculator's own worked example: 12 × 10 × 4 in slab, 44 ft³ with 10 percent, 1.63 yd³, 74 bags of 80 lb), the lines around it, the work and what goes in writing. Short-load charges point to the Jefferson County source the bags-or-truck guide already cites; every price is left to the reader's suppliers. Spanish terms follow the concrete calculator (losa, zapata, colado).
3. **Copy pending** the owner's reading (`ownerReview: pending`).
4. **Also archives** `guide-links` (merged in #165) and syncs [GU6], [GU7]; adds [GU8] to the main spec directly, as this change is what makes it true.
