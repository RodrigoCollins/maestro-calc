# English-Spanish construction glossary

The words of the job in English and in Spanish, by trade, with what each one means. They are the words Maestro Calc uses on every calculator and guide, so a crew, a client and a store can read the same numbers in either language.

Some words change from one country to another. The Spanish column gives the one we use on this site, the one most of our readers hear in stores and on site in the US.
