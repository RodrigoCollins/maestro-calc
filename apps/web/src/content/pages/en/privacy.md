# Privacy

This page describes what Maestro Calc does with information today. It covers only what the site does now.

## Hosting

Maestro Calc is hosted on Cloudflare. When you load a page, Cloudflare processes standard request logs, which can include your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser type.

## Cookies and tracking

Maestro Calc does not use cookies. The site has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts, and it does not keep anything about you in your browser. Your browser may send a Do Not Track signal. The site does no tracking, so the signal changes nothing.

## Calculators

Calculators run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us. The values you enter sit in the page address, so you can copy the link and share it. Opening or sharing that address sends it like any page request, so it can appear in Cloudflare's request logs. The address with your values also stays in your browser history, like any page you visit.

## Email

If you write to contacto@maestrocalc.com, we use your message and your address only to reply to you.

## Changes

We will update this page before we add analytics or advertising.
